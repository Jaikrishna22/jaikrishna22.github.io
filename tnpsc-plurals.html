1. Basic Concept of Number

What is Number?

Number is a grammatical category that shows whether a noun refers to one person/thing (singular) or more than one (plural).

Singular vs Plural

Singular Plural Example
One person/thing More than one cat → cats
Takes singular verb (is, was, has) Takes plural verb (are, were, have) The cat is black | The cats are black
✅ Examples:
• The book is on the table. (Singular)
• The books are on the table. (Plural)
• A student has homework. (Singular)
• Students have homework. (Plural)
💡 Remember: Plural usually means "more than one," but sometimes one word with many units is plural (e.g., "scissors," "trousers").

2. Regular Plural Rules

Rule 1: Add -S

Most nouns form the plural by adding -s

SingularPlural
bookbooks
penpens
catcats
dogdogs

Rule 2: Add -ES (Words ending in S, SS, SH, CH, X, Z, O)

Add -es when the word ends in: s, ss, sh, ch, x, z, o

EndingSingularPlural
-sbusbuses
-ssclassclasses
-shdishdishes
-chwatchwatches
-xboxboxes
-zbuzzbuzzes
-oheroheroes
⭐ EXCEPTIONS: photo → photos, piano → pianos, tomato → tomatoes

Rule 3: Consonant + Y → Change Y to -IES

If a word ends in consonant + y, change the y to -ies

SingularPlural
babybabies
citycities
ladyladies
familyfamilies

Rule 4: Vowel + Y → Just Add -S

If a word ends in vowel + y, just add -s

SingularPlural
boyboys
toytoys
keykeys
daydays

Rule 5: F/FE → Change to -VES

Words ending in f or fe usually change to -ves

SingularPlural
leafleaves
knifeknives
wifewives
lifelives
shelfshelves
⭐ EXCEPTIONS: roof → roofs, chief → chiefs, safe → safes

4. Uncountable Nouns (Always Plural or No Plural)

Uncountable Nouns (Take Singular Verb)

These nouns cannot be counted and don't have a plural form:

NounExampleWhy Uncountable?
informationThe information is useful.Abstract, many items together
adviceHis advice was helpful.Abstract concept
furnitureThe furniture is expensive.Collection of items
equipmentThe equipment works well.Collection of tools
luggageThe luggage is heavy.Collection of bags
baggageThe baggage was lost.Collection of bags
knowledgeKnowledge is power.Abstract concept
newsThe news is good.Information
sceneryThe scenery is beautiful.General landscape
❌ WRONG: "The informations are..."
✅ CORRECT: "The information is..."

❌ WRONG: "Can I have some advices?"
✅ CORRECT: "Can I have some advice?" OR "Can I have a piece of advice?"

Making Uncountable Nouns Countable

Use a "counter" word before the uncountable noun:

UncountableCountable Version
informationa piece of information / two pieces of information
advicea piece of advice / two pieces of advice
furniturea piece of furniture / three pieces of furniture
luggagea piece of luggage / two suitcases
baggagea bag of baggage / three bags
