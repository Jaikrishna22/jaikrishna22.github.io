About Me

Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India · Senior Software Engineer @ Perpetuuiti Technosoft

I'm a self-motivated IT professional with 8+ years of proven experience across the .NET and Angular/React stacks, building secure, user-friendly web applications and enterprise tools.

My core specialisation is Disaster Recovery (DR), Business Continuity Planning (BCP) and IT Disaster Recovery (ITDR) — I design, implement and automate DR solutions for enterprise clients across Telecom, Banking, Financial Services, Insurance and Retail. That includes hands-on failover, switchover and switchback drills tuned to strict RTO/RPO targets, across operating systems, databases, virtualization, cloud, storage, networking and replication technologies.

I like breaking complex requirements into clean architectural components, practise test-driven development, and enjoy leading mixed teams of junior and senior engineers through end-to-end delivery, code reviews and architecture decisions.
