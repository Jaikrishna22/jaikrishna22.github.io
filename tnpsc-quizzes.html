✍️ Sentence Correction (25 Questions)

1. Easy

🔍 Error Spotting (20 Questions)

Find the grammatical error in each sentence:

1. Easy

He have completed his homework and have submitted it to the teacher.

✅ Error: "He have" should be "He has" (subject-verb agreement). Third person singular takes "has", not "have".
2. Easy

The information are very important for our research project.

✅ "Information" is an uncountable noun and always takes a singular verb. "are" should be "is".
3. Medium

Despite of his hard work, he did not pass the examination.

✅ Error: "Despite of" is incorrect. Use either "Despite" (preposition) or "In spite of" (prepositional phrase). "Despite of" is redundant.
4. Medium

She suggested that he should go to the doctor immediately.

✅ No error. "Suggest" is correctly followed by should go (or just "go"). This sentence is grammatically correct.
5. Hard

Neither the students nor the teacher were willing to attend the meeting.

✅ Error: With "neither...nor", the verb agrees with the noun closest to it. "teacher" is singular, so use "was" not "were".

💡 One-word Substitution (25 Questions)

Replace the underlined phrase with a single word:

1. A person who collects coins and stamps as a hobby: Easy
✅ Philatelist = collector of stamps. (Numismatist = collector of coins, but both are needed here)
2. A person who writes for newspapers and magazines: Easy
✅ Journalist = person who writes for newspapers/magazines and gathers news.
3. A disease that spreads rapidly and affects many people: Medium
✅ Epidemic = disease spreading widely in a region. (Pandemic = spreads across countries/continents)
4. Someone who leads an expedition to unexplored regions: Medium
✅ Explorer = person who travels to unexplored regions to discover new places/things.
5. A strong desire to eat too much food: Hard
✅ Gluttony = excessive eating; the practice of eating too much. It's a sin/vice.
